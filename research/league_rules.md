# League rules: dated timeline and current settings

Reconstructed from league email and the commissioner's Drive documents,
2010–2026. Every row is anchored to a quote. This is the reference for
`config/league.yaml`; where the two disagree, fix the config.

**Standing meta-rule:** six votes change a rule; silence keeps the status quo.
*"Needs five other ayes to pass — default without a quorum is always that the
previous season's rules continue"* (Cannon, 2022-07-30). So **2026 inherits
2025's rules** unless a vote happened on GroupMe, where league business moved in
2024 and which is not readable from email.

## Current settings (2025, carried into 2026)

- **10 teams**, snake draft, **15 rounds**, 7 bench spots.
- **Starters (8):** QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, FLEX, FLEX (FLEX = RB/WR/TE).
  **No kicker, no DST.**
- **Regular season: 14 weeks**, dual points — 28 available. Tiebreak: H2H wins,
  then total points.
- **Playoffs: 4 teams, no byes.** Week 15 is a one-week semifinal; **weeks 16–17
  are a two-week aggregate final.** *"1 week matchup week 15 and two week
  championship weeks 16-17. No game week 18, as always."* (Cannon, 2024-08-24).
  The #1 seed picks its semifinal opponent.
- **Consolation:** seeds 5–10 play a loser's bracket over weeks 15–17.
- **Scoring:** half PPR (0.5), **passing TD = 6**, rush/rec TD = 6.
- **Buy-in $200.** No keepers, no auction.
